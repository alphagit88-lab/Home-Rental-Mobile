Home Rent Account Deletion

Last updated: May 6, 2026

You can request deletion of your Home Rent account and associated app data by emailing alphagit88@gmail.com .

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address linked to your Home Rent account.
  2. Use the subject line “Home Rent account deletion request”.
  3. Include your full name and the role used in the app.

What will be deleted

  • Account profile details stored for Home Rent.
  • Associated listing, booking, or role-based app records that are eligible for deletion.
  • Locally stored session data on your device after you sign out or reinstall the app.

What may be retained

Certain records may be retained when needed for fraud prevention, dispute handling, security, or legal compliance.

Questions

Contact alphagit88@gmail.com if you need help with your request.
