Home Rent Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Home Rent helps tenants, property owners, and service providers manage property listings, bookings, and account details from a mobile app.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as your name, email address, password, and role.
  • Profile and listing information that you submit in the app.
  • Booking details, stay dates, guest counts, and related property data.
  • Location details that are added to properties or used for map previews.
  • Support messages or issue reports that you send to us.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To show listings, bookings, availability, and account content.
  • To support map features and property location previews.
  • To respond to support requests and improve app reliability.

Data storage and sharing

Some data is stored on your device, including signed-in session data that helps you stay logged in. Information submitted through the app may also be processed by Home Rent backend services. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

The current app experience collects booking payment details for booking submission flows. If payment processing changes in the future, this policy should be updated to reflect the live payment provider and the specific data handling used in production.

Your choices

You can request access updates or deletion of your account data by using the account deletion page linked from the app or by contacting alphagit88@gmail.com.
